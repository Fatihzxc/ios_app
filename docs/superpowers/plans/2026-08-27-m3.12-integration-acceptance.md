# M3.12 Integration, Accessibility, Privacy and Evidence Plan

> Execute on top of accepted M3.11 SHA `2e021ffadfebcd7c3799994998d0fbc49e94862c`. Keep the M3.11 branch immutable. The M3.12 implementation uses one test-only RED commit amended to GREEN, followed by a separate evidence-gate RED commit amended to GREEN.

**Goal:** Close the remaining M3 integration gaps without duplicating the already accepted M3.1-M3.11 repository, persistence, safety, photo, CloudKit-fake, and notification-fake coverage.

**Architecture:** `AppRootView` remains the owner of cross-feature routing. The cached `AppDependencies` tracker factory remains lazy. `TrackerFeatureBundle` composes app-owned Progress quick actions without creating feature-to-feature imports. `ProgressPhotosKit` owns a pure broader-library-access policy and the real system picker consumes that policy. A focused M3 acceptance verifier owns privacy, integration, screenshot, small-phone, and evidence contracts.

**Acceptance boundary:** Simulator tests may prove picker availability and protocol-fake CloudKit/notification behavior, but they may not claim a real photo selection, notification delivery, signed CloudKit transfer, or physical-device behavior.
